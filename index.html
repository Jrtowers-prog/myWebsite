About me

I'm Jamie, a Computer Science student specialising in software engineering at the University of Plymouth. This website is here as it provides the freedom to present myself in the method I want, not through another preconfigured platform. Also, it's a fun little side project to work on when I don't want to face the real work.

I'm someone that likes to try lot's of different things. Since learning Spanish to live in Barcelona, I've developed a keen interest in languages, right now I'm learning Arabic (Egyptian dialect).

I also like to build things. It could be a project, a business or even a new skill. Having an overarching goal fills me with purpose and direction. A 1% improvement every day for a year allows you to make big changes.

If anyone is looking for an open-minded, driven and communicative graduate to wear many hats, please get in touch.

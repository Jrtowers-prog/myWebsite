Introduction

Jamie · 20 November 2026

If you've clicked here, you probably have an idea of what this is, although it may not be clear what to expect. This is going to be a space for my writing, whatever it ends up being about. There will be no strictly-bound topics or themes, what will be present here will be whatever I feel like writing.

I have wanted to start writing for a little while. It feels good to write for whatver reason. Maybe it is that it allows you to more easily string together thoughts than if you tried to follow them along in your head alone. Setting up this page and posting some thoughts has made it easier to start writing. There is a good chance this get's taken down or atleast modified as it is slightly nerveracking having my thoughts online. However for the time being, this is going to stay public.

How I will structure each post is currently a mystery to me. Writing about topics of interest could be a strong exercise in testing how thoroughly I understand what I believe I do. This also sounds like it could be fun sometimes and at other times draining.

There is not much more to say here for now, however if you check this page repeatedly, you will likely notice that it will change a lot due to future revisions I am betting I will make.

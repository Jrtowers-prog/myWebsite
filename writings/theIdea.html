The Idea

Jamie · 20 November 2026

After months of turning over different ideas, the decision to commit to one has been made. The core concept: A proximity-based blocking device that removes distractions around your designated deep working area. What this actually means is that there will be a device that uses a Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) signal in combination with a mobile app to block selected distracing applications and notifications, allowing someone to immerse themselves in effective deep work. This concept already exists and is used in a multitude of different devices such as Brick Night, Halo or the Busy Bar. There are problems with each such as Brick Night being too expensive, Halo being available in the US only and targeted at bedtime, or the Busy Bar being overwhelming.

What happens next is the scary part. There are features that need to be proven to work or else the entire idea will collapse. This means that this is where the technical research begins. The objective of the technical research is to create a theoretical proof of concept for the basic functionality of the device. For example, theoretically prooving that it is possible to create automatic proximity-based blocking and that a phone cannot easily escape this. AI will be useful in this process as it can be used, when prompted correctly, to trawl through vast amounts of technical content to pull useful information. As previously mentioned, AI is only useful when instructed carefully and with thorough detail. I will have to be very careful to use it in a way that it does not provide information to confirm my biases but rather information that objectively measures the situation.

Once the initial technical research has been concluded, prototyping will begin. This prototyping is to turn the theoretical proof of concept into a tangible one.

Now the research is beginning, I will report back once progress has been made.

Some on-the-go thoughts whilst brainstorming for a project

Jamie · 20 November 2026

I'm in the process of deciding what I want to create for my final year project in university. I have found this to be a difficult process as there are lot's of compelling problems, but to narrow them down is difficult.

What I'm thinking about now is a company that I came across roughly a month ago named OffPrint. The purpose of the company is to allow users to send in blogs, articles and other formations of informaton that they find interesting, the OffPrint collates these into a physical and nicely-designed monthly article. I like this concept as it allows people to look away from the screens whilst creating accountability for what people have claimed interest in. The program costs roughly $30 per month, which is a hefty price for a magazine. This means that the information in that magazine is more likely to be read and forces people to stick to the idealogical guidelines they set for themselves.

I like this concept and am currently thinking about a system that helps people engage more deeply with topics that interest them. This is difficult when people are currently stuck in a system that prioritises fleeting, surface-level engagement. Firstly, in today's day and age, do people even have topics of interest? Is my attraction to this concept driven by a biased view that everyone else wants to free their brains from the shackles of modern technology and engage in real learning, or is it a belief based in fact? Even if it is based in fact and people want to learn to learn and do truely have an urge to develop the skills such as critical thinking that only come from intentional learning, not ultra-supervised drip-feeding, how are we to develop a system to capture this?

It could be simple. If we ignore obvious flaws like engagement and being obsolete, a system that performs this purpose is easy to think of. For example, people enter in a topic of interest and the system brings back relevant, reputable material on the topic. Another word for this is a library. There are clear problems with this as a project. It is not solving any clear problem, it is not novel in any way and it is incredibly vague. How can this be tweaked to be more applicable to the problems faced today?s

What is it about a library versus the current system that is causing people to not engage in active learning the way they used to? I believe that there is a large population of people that want to engage in learning and generally, have interests, that are not doing so, why? Social media and short-form content has captivated and hijacked our brains. The constant flooding of entertainment has made it harder for us to sustain focus and engage in learning, as it takes time and concentration, even if it is an area of interest. This is not the only problem, in recent years the necessity to self-study has been diminished due to the advancement of AI. This has intellectually de-skilled us and impacted the efficacy of education. To pass you no longer need to know the knowledge needed to pass, you just need the help of AI that can hold the knowledge for you.

What are the issues that need solving for this to become a system used today by those who want help? - People don't always know what they are interested in. Take money, given them 3 days to figure out what they want to look into. - The shortform content is more engaging, making it difficult to stay consistent with learning. Make it habitual, even if the user get's distracted, they come back to it without thinking. This could mean that they have to complete this task before they can access the enjoyable, distracting applications. - It is easy to get distracted from learning as it required mental work. Payment keeps people accountable.
